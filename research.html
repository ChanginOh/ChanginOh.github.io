Research Interests

My research combines mathematical modelling, numerical simulation, and computational methods to investigate complex biological and molecular systems. I am particularly interested in mathematical oncology, scientific machine learning, optimization, and geometric methods, with an emphasis on developing interpretable approaches for biological applications.

  • Mathematical Oncology
  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Global Optimization
  • Geometric Learning
  • Coding Theory

Current Directions

Age-dependent tumour-immune modelling

My work develops mechanistic mathematical models of tumour growth and immune response to investigate how aging alters immune recruitment, cytotoxic activity, and tumour clearance. Ultimately, I aim to use these models to explain age-dependent patterns of cancer incidence.

Optimization for parameter estimation

Biological models often involve nonlinear, weakly identifiable, and constrained parameters. My work focuses on developing robust global optimization methods for estimating model parameters from sparse biological data.

Information-theoretic inference from interfacial solvation

My work develops an information-theoretic framework for inferring surface properties from the molecular structure of interfacial solvents. By viewing surface-solvent interactions as a noisy communication process, the framework quantifies how much information solvent orientations carry about a surface.
